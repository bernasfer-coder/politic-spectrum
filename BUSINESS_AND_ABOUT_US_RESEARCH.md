# Business entity, jurisdiction, and About Us research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-12. This is a planning document, not legal, tax, accounting, or immigration advice. The goal is legal tax optimization and a trustworthy public presence—not hiding ownership, management, income, or data processing.

## Preliminary answer

Do not choose Brazil, Switzerland, or the United States from the headline corporate-tax rate alone. The likely best jurisdiction is normally the place where the founder actually lives, works, manages the project, and can maintain the entity. An overseas company can create a second tax residence, permanent-establishment, controlled-foreign-company, reporting, withholding, banking, VAT/sales-tax, and compliance problem.

Do not incorporate solely because the site may eventually earn advertising revenue. First validate traffic and monetization. Form an entity when revenue, contracts, liability, payment processors, sponsorships, intellectual-property ownership, or an investor requires it. Before incorporation, obtain written advice from a licensed accountant/tax lawyer in the founder’s actual tax-residence country and a lawyer for the chosen entity’s country.

## Decision inputs required

- Founder’s citizenship, tax residence, and expected place of work/management.
- Whether the founder will be resident in Brazil, Switzerland, the US, or another country at launch.
- Expected gross revenue and net profit at 0–50k, 50–250k, and 250k+ annual equivalents.
- Revenue mix: display ads, direct sponsorships, donations, grants, subscriptions, affiliate income, licensing, or consulting.
- Whether profits will be distributed or reinvested.
- Whether there will be employees, co-founders, investors, contractors, or a US customer/investor requirement.
- Where the IP, domain, trademarks, bank/payment accounts, hosting contracts, and ad-network contracts should be owned.
- Target markets and whether the business will collect personal data or political-profile data. The current product recommendation is not to collect those profiles.
- Required privacy, consumer-protection, VAT/GST/sales-tax, accounting, bookkeeping, insurance, and annual-reporting obligations.

## Jurisdiction comparison to complete

### Switzerland

Switzerland is the strongest default candidate if the founder lives and effectively manages the company there. The Swiss Federal Tax Administration says legal entities headquartered or effectively managed in Switzerland are generally subject to Swiss tax by personal affiliation. Federal profit tax is 8.5% of net profit, with additional cantonal and communal taxes; the exact burden depends on the canton and commune. The normal Swiss VAT rate is 8.1%, and the VAT registration analysis depends on taxable turnover and other facts.

Research a Swiss GmbH/Sàrl versus sole proprietorship, including capital, director/residency requirements, bookkeeping, payroll/social contributions, withholding tax on distributions, cantonal choice, IP ownership, and the cost of annual accounting. A low-tax canton is not automatically usable if the actual management and work occur elsewhere.

### Brazil

Brazil is the strongest default candidate if the founder lives and works in Brazil and the business is genuinely operated there. The official DREI guidance describes the Sociedade Limitada as a common limited-liability form that can have one or more partners, requires accounting support, and may qualify for Simples Nacional subject to the applicable rules. Official 2026 guidance confirms that the Simples National framework and its limits/sub-limits are being affected by consumption-tax reform, so calculations must use the current activity classification and tax tables rather than a generic internet estimate.

Research SLU/Ltda, ME/EPP, Simples Nacional annex/activity classification, ISS/IBS/CBS transition, export and foreign-payment treatment, bookkeeping, pró-labore/social contributions, dividend treatment, payment processors, and Brazilian data/privacy obligations. Confirm whether advertising, sponsorship, donations, and software/content services fall under the same tax treatment; they may not.

### United States

The US is not automatically more profitable for a non-US founder. The IRS explains that an LLC may be treated as a corporation, partnership, or disregarded entity depending on elections and ownership. A US C corporation generally calculates federal income tax at 21%, before state taxes, compliance costs, and taxes or withholding at the owner level. The IRS also notes that foreign-owned US disregarded entities can have Form 5472 and pro-forma Form 1120 reporting obligations, and foreign persons commonly face 30% withholding on certain US-source income unless a treaty or other rule reduces it.

Research Delaware and other state choices only if there is a concrete reason such as US investors, US employees, or material US operations. Compare LLC, C corporation, and an entity in the founder’s home country; include state franchise fees, registered agent, annual reports, federal/state filings, banking, payment processing, US sales-tax exposure, IP, treaty treatment, owner-country anti-deferral/CFC rules, and the cost of moving profits to the founder.

## Profit model

For each candidate, calculate:

`gross revenue − ad/payment fees − hosting/software − contractors/payroll − accounting/legal/insurance − entity taxes − indirect taxes not recoverable − withholding − owner-level taxes − currency/banking costs = founder’s net cash`

Run at least three scenarios: low/no revenue, profitable and mostly distributed, and profitable but reinvested. Include the cost of maintaining an entity during a bad year. Do not treat VAT/sales tax collected from customers as profit. Record assumptions and dates so the result can be rerun when rates change.

## About Us page: recommended scope

Add a public, accessible About Us page as soon as the project is public, even before incorporation. It should contain:

- project name, mission, and the problem the tool addresses;
- the people or organization operating it, with a contact method that does not expose a private home address unless a specific law requires it;
- country/jurisdiction of the operator, and legal entity/registration details once an entity exists;
- non-partisanship/editorial-independence statement only if it is accurate;
- explanation that the six-dimensional results are illustrative analysis, not a diagnosis, vote recommendation, or definitive classification;
- methodology, revision date, version, limitations, and link to the research/source policy;
- corrections/contact process and a source-quality policy;
- sponsorship and advertising policy, including sponsor review, disclosure, and the rule that sponsors cannot purchase result-based targeting;
- privacy, cookie/consent, data-retention, and clear-local-data links;
- accessibility, language, security-contact, and uptime/status links where applicable.

The About Us page must not imply an incorporated company, research institution, academic endorsement, or political neutrality that does not exist. Keep legal operator details, privacy-controller details, and editorial claims synchronized.

## Legal and operational checks before receiving money

- [ ] Confirm who is the legal operator and tax resident today.
- [ ] Confirm whether an individual can lawfully receive the intended ad/sponsor payments before incorporation.
- [ ] Obtain tax-residence and cross-border advice before selecting a foreign entity.
- [ ] Verify ad-network eligibility, publisher contracts, payment onboarding, and payout-country restrictions.
- [ ] Separate business banking and bookkeeping from personal finances.
- [ ] Register and document ownership of domain, code, content, trademarks, and research data.
- [ ] Obtain appropriate liability/cyber/media insurance quotes.
- [ ] Confirm consumer, advertising, sponsorship disclosure, VAT/GST/sales-tax, and invoice requirements.
- [ ] Confirm privacy-controller/processor roles and international transfer mechanisms for every vendor.
- [ ] Ensure no questionnaire answers, scores, political labels, or inferred political opinions are sent to a company, ad network, analytics provider, or buyer.

## Deliverables / acceptance criteria

- [ ] Founder fact sheet containing tax residence, work location, citizenship, and ownership assumptions.
- [ ] Written advice from qualified professionals in the founder’s actual tax-residence country.
- [ ] Decision matrix for no entity/individual, Brazil SLU/Ltda, Swiss GmbH/Sàrl, US LLC, and US C corporation.
- [ ] Three-year cash-flow model with low, base, and high revenue scenarios; distributed and reinvested-profit cases.
- [ ] Total-cost comparison including formation, annual compliance, accounting, tax, payment, banking, currency, and closure costs.
- [ ] Cross-border risk memo covering effective management, permanent establishment, CFC/anti-deferral, withholding, VAT/GST/sales tax, and IP ownership.
- [ ] Written recommendation with the reason, assumptions, alternatives rejected, trigger to revisit, and exit plan.
- [ ] About Us page implemented with accurate operator, mission, methodology, sources, privacy, corrections, advertising, and sponsorship information.
- [ ] Approval gate: no company formation, ad contracts, or political-data monetization until the comparison is reviewed and approved.
