Reviewed 2026-09-16

GEOGRAPHIC DATA
Natural Earth 4.1.0, Admin 0 countries, 1:110m, distributed through
world-atlas 2.0.2 (countries-110m.json). Simplified, fixed reference geometry;
not a real-time control map or a historical boundary reconstruction.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/disputed-boundaries-policy/
https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas

Natural Earth declares its vector and raster data public domain, including
modification and commercial use. Data authors: Tom Patterson, Nathaniel
Vaughn Kelso and contributors. No accuracy or fitness guarantee is implied.
The app projects this geometry with Equal Earth. It adds case-based colors
and rounded editorial place anchors; those are navigation aids, not borders.
Dataset names and territorial divisions do not establish sovereignty.
